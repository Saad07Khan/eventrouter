EventRouter

One event, fanned out to three destinations. The interesting part is not the happy path — it is that one destination fails, retries on its own schedule and dead-letters without the other two noticing. Retry state lives per destination, not per event.

Creates a source, wires up three destinations, sends one event, then polls GET /v1/events/{id} every two seconds.
